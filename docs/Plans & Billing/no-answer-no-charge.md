---
title: No answer = no charge
---
This isn't a marketing line. It's how the system is wired.

A check costs you something when the call ends in a definitive answer: a clear in stock, not in, sold out, or doesn't carry it. It also costs you when a real person picked up and spent their time on us, even if we walked away without an answer.

If nobody ever picked up, it's free, automatically. Nobody answered. The line was busy. A bad number. The store turned out closed. The call broke on our end. You keep your check, every time.

One thing to be straight about. Left on hold. Too slammed to check. We couldn't understand each other. They hung up on us. Those are charged, because somebody at that store really did stop what they were doing for you, and their minutes cost us either way.

> 🛡️ **You pay when a person picks up. Never when nobody does.**
>
> The charge is decided by the server after the call, on the call record alone. A line that never got answered can't quietly cost you.
